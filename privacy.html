Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25 September 2026

Introduction

Jordan Ross ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SWAGFLIP Parkour Origins mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

When you use SWAGFLIP, we may collect the following types of information:

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Advertising IDs: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA) for ad serving and attribution
  • Usage Data: Game progress, levels completed, in-app actions, and gameplay statistics
  • Technical Data: Crash logs, performance data, and diagnostic information to improve app stability

Optional Information

  • Information you provide when contacting support
  • Purchase history for in-app purchases (processed through your app store)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App functionality
  • Personalize your gaming experience
  • Display relevant advertisements through our ad partners
  • Analyze app performance and user behavior to improve the game
  • Detect and prevent technical issues, bugs, and crashes
  • Respond to support requests and customer inquiries

Advertising and Third-Party Services

SWAGFLIP uses Yodo1 Mobile Advertising Mediation and other third-party advertising networks to display ads within the App. These ad partners may collect and use information about your device and app usage to serve personalized advertisements.

Our advertising partners may use technologies such as cookies, advertising IDs, and similar tracking technologies. These partners have their own privacy policies governing their data collection and use practices.

Key advertising partners include:

  • Yodo1 MAS (Mediation as a Service)
  • Google AdMob
  • AppLovin
  • Unity Ads
  • Other ad networks listed in our app-ads.txt file

For more information about how our ad partners use your data, please review:

  • Yodo1 Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

SWAGFLIP Parkour Origins is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below, and we will delete such information from our systems.

Note: This policy assumes the app is rated for general audiences and is not specifically designed for children. If your app is directed at children or uses age-gated features, additional COPPA and GDPR-K compliance measures may be required.

Data Retention

We retain collected information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services and as required by applicable laws. Device and usage data are typically retained for analytical purposes and may be aggregated or anonymized for long-term storage.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can limit ad tracking through your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Advertising; Android: Settings > Google > Ads)
  • Access and Deletion: You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us
  • Withdraw Consent: Where we process data based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
